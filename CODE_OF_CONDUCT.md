# Code of Conduct

Topoli follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: showing empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, harassment in public or private, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via GitHub's **Report content** feature on any comment, or by opening a confidential security advisory on this repository. Reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
